Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take for online payments to reflect?

Payments made via E-Wallet (GCash / Maya) and Debit/Credit card reflect immediately in your account balance and payment history log. Over-The-Counter payments at branch tellers process within 1 business hour.

Is there a transaction processing fee?

A minimal flat fee of ₱15.00 is charged by electronic payment gateways per successful transaction. Over-the-counter payments directly at our Batangas Main Branch carry ₱0 convenience fees.

Where can I request official BIR tax receipts for business loans?

Digital receipts generated through this portal are valid for proof of payment. For BIR-stamped physical Official Receipts (OR), visit our Evangelista St., Batangas City branch or email request to alaebank@gmail.com with your transaction reference number.
